Who We Are

Premium Mobile Cleaning Services in Liverpool

Clean Ninja is a trusted, professional cleaning company delivering high-quality services across Liverpool and surrounding areas. We specialise in a wide range of solutions including mobile car valeting, home deep cleaning, end of tenancy cleaning, window cleaning, and pavement cleaning.

Our goal is simple — we don’t just clean, we transform spaces. Whether it’s restoring the shine to your vehicle, bringing freshness back to your home, or preparing a property for its next tenant, our experienced team ensures every job is completed to the highest standard.

With fully equipped mobile units and a commitment to reliability, we bring convenience right to your doorstep. Clean Ninja combines professional expertise with eco-friendly practices to deliver results you can trust every time.
